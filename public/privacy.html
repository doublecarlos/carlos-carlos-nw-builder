Privacy

Carlos Carlos' NW Builder - updated 30 August 2026

Your builds

Builds, layers, custom items and settings are saved by your own browser, in IndexedDB and a few keys beginning with nw:. Nothing is uploaded - there is no server and no account. Export is the only way data leaves your machine, and you do that yourself.

"Import from game" reads tooltips out of a screenshot. That happens in your browser; the image is never uploaded.

What the host sees

Cloudflare Pages serves the site and keeps ordinary request logs - IP address, time, page, user-agent - to deliver it and stop abuse. Browsers may also report failed requests to Cloudflare. I have no analytics and never look at individual visitors.

What is not here

  • No cookies, so nothing to consent to.
  • No analytics, ads, accounts or tracking.
  • No third-party services - everything loads from this site.
  • Nothing sold or shared.

Deleting it

Delete builds in the app, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. Export anything you want to keep first.

Questions

If something here is wrong or unclear, open an issue on the project's tracker.
